Hard / Advanced Commands

Real techniques used by professionals

Firewall Evasion - Fragmentation

nmap -sS -f -T2 -p 80,443 192.168.1.10

Sends tiny fragmented packets to try bypassing simple firewalls.

Decoy Scan

nmap -sS -D RND:10,ME -p 22,80,443 target.com

Hides your IP among 10 random decoys + your real IP.

Idle / Zombie Scan

nmap -sI zombie_ip target.com -p 80,443

Completely blind scan using a zombie host (advanced).

Full Aggressive + Scripts

nmap -A -T4 -p- --script=vuln,exploit target.com -oA fullscan

Very noisy but extremely informative.

Slow & Stealthy

nmap -sS -T1 --max-rate 10 -p 1-1000 -f target.com

Very slow, fragmented, low rate — good against IDS.

UDP + Version Detection

nmap -sU -sV --version-intensity 5 -p 53,161,500,1194 target.com

Harder and slower than TCP, but necessary for many services.
